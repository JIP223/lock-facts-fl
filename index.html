Florida locksmith facts

There is no Florida locksmith license to look up. Here is what you can still verify.

Lock Facts FL explains the law, the $19 bait-and-switch, and — when we can open an official record — the legal name of a business. We do not endorse locksmiths or invent license numbers.

Not a recommendation or endorsement. Any business names on this site come from a public-records lookup: the Florida Division of Corporations (Sunbiz) listed that exact entity as Active on the date we checked. That is not a locksmith license, a rating, or a claim that the business is insured, skilled, or fairly priced. Verify the business yourself before you hire anyone.
Important correction to a common assumption. Florida does not issue a statewide locksmith license. Miami-Dade County used to license locksmiths, but the county announced that it stopped, effective July 1, 2025, because state law preempted that local license. Details are on the Miami-Dade page.
