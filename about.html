About & methodology

Lock Facts FL is an independent information site. Listing a name is not a recommendation, a rating, or a guarantee of workmanship, insurance, or price.

What we will publish

  • Statements about licensing only when we can cite an official statute or government page we opened.
  • Business names only when we opened an official Florida Division of Corporations (Sunbiz) search result showing that exact name, document number, and status — or another official public source.
  • Addresses only when they appear on an official record or on the business’s own site that we opened. If we cannot open the detail record, we omit the address rather than guess.
  • The check date. Current pages were checked November 18, 2026, America/New_York.

What we will not publish

  • Invented license numbers, prices, or “verified insured” claims.
  • Star ratings or “best of” language.
  • Any implication that a Sunbiz filing is a locksmith license. It is not. It only means a business entity by that name is on file with the state.

If you are locked out, get a firm total price in writing before work starts, and do not rely on this site as a substitute for your own checks.
